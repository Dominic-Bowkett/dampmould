Damp, mould and HHSRS are exactly the kind of subjects best learned from someone
who **does the work** — not just teaches the manual. This page explains what good
training looks like and how to choose, and lets you register interest in
practitioner-led courses.

> **General information.** Course details and enrolment links will be published
> here. New to the subject? Start with [HHSRS explained](/hhsrs).

## Who these courses are for

- **Landlords and property managers** who want to understand their exposure and
  **evidence compliance** under [Awaab's Law](/awaabs-law).
- **Housing and surveying professionals** sharpening their HHSRS and damp
  diagnosis skills.
- **Career-changers** moving into housing compliance and surveying — see
  [how to become a damp and mould surveyor](/become-a-damp-mould-surveyor).

## What good training covers

- **Causes and diagnosis** of damp and mould — condensation, penetrating and
  rising damp, and building performance.
- **[HHSRS](/hhsrs) assessment** — hazards, scoring, and the Category 1/2
  distinction.
- **The Awaab's Law landscape** — duties, timescales and evidencing compliance.
- **Practical application** — worked examples and real-world judgement, not just
  slides.
- **Defensible recording** — the evidence that stands up later.

## How to choose a course

- **A credible, practising trainer** with genuine field experience.
- **An up-to-date syllabus** reflecting current guidance and Awaab's Law.
- **The right depth** for your role and goals.
- **A practical element** — these subjects are learned by doing.
- **Recognition** (such as CPD) if you need it.
